Biography

Faten Anis is a Partner in Matouk Bassiouny & Hennawy's Finance and Projects practice group and is qualified in both Egypt and France. Before joining MBH, she spent over a decade at the leading Paris firm Gide Loyrette Nouel. She advises lenders and borrowers, including credit and multilateral institutions, debt funds, corporates and SPVs, on project and real estate finance, LBOs, bond issuances, corporate financing, and debt structuring and restructuring.
