Biography

Tamer El Hennawy is a Partner at Matouk Bassiouny & Hennawy, the Co-Head of the Corporate and M&A, and Capital Markets practice groups, and heads the firm’s real estate sector. He represents leading Egyptian corporations and has been working on most of the public offerings that occurred in the country in the past six years.
